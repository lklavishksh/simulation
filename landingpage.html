****** This is a Phishing Simulation ******

You just clicked on a test link — but in a real scenario, this could have been harmful.

Here’s why this matters:

  • Phishing emails often look legitimate — using real names, logos, or familiar topics (like internal projects or apps).
  • Clicking unknown links can compromise our systems, steal login credentials, or install malware.
  • Always take a moment to think before clicking:
    • Were you expecting this email?
    • Is the sender’s email address legitimate?
    • Is the link suspicious or unexpected?
    • Does the message ask for credentials or sensitive info?

What you should do instead:

  • Hover over links to check where they go.
  • Report anything suspicious to Nuvollo immediately.
  • When in doubt — don’t click!
